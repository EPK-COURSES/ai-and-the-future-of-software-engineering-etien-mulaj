This research is honestly such a huge reality check. It turns out AI isn't nearly as perfect as everyone on TikTok makes it seem. The most surprising part to me is that even though AI writes code super fast, it actually creates a ton of extra work. Because it just copies random stuff from the internet, it ends up making super messy code with hidden bugs. Senior devs are out here spending way too much time fixing AI mistakes and cleaning up its mess instead of just doing their actual work. It's crazy that a tool meant to save time can sometimes slow things down so much.
The stuff about junior developers is definitely the scariest part of the whole thing. New grads usually start out with easy coding tasks to learn the ropes and get experience. But since AI can do all that basic stuff now, it feels like companies won't even want to hire beginners anymore. It's like, how is anyone supposed to get a foot in the door if there are no entry-level jobs left? Plus, there's a big worry that people will just get lazy, copy-paste everything from ChatGPT, and never actually learn how to think for themselves.

But there are some cool parts to it too. If AI does all the boring, repetitive coding and catches basic typos, developers can focus on the fun stuff like problem-solving and designing cool features. You can also use AI to build your own apps and side projects way faster than before.

Honestly, this totally changes how the future of tech looks. It’s not about AI stealing everyone's jobs, it's just changing what the job actually is. Just knowing how to write lines of code isn't enough anymore. Future engineers have to be good at the big-picture stuff, double-checking the AI, and actually talking to people. It's definitely a little scary, but it's also pretty exciting.
